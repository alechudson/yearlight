# Weather deck

## Design stance
Treat the lower section as a miniature weather instrument.

## Key choices
Time and date form the header. An oversized yellow temperature is the hero below; a pixel icon, condition, and today’s high/low sit beside it.

## Trade-offs
The most useful outdoors, but time is smaller than in the other concepts.

## Best for
Deciding what to wear or whether to head outside.

## Interaction
Switch clear/rain/wide/no-weather samples; compare 12/24-hour time and native/2× pixels. These are browser-only controls.
