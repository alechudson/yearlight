CONCEPT 01

Observatory

Quiet · centered · time first

One centered stack, with nothing competing for attention.

The cleanest everyday read, but gives up forecast detail.

CONCEPT 02

Split-flap

Architectural · asymmetric · organized

Separate time, calendar, and weather into three deliberate zones.

Fast scanning and strong structure; the weather ribbon is more visually assertive.

CONCEPT 03

Weather deck

Practical · weather first · bold

Treat the lower section as a miniature weather instrument.

The most useful outdoors, but time is smaller than in the other concepts.

CONCEPT 04

Almanac

Calendar led · graphic · compact

Make the calendar a graphic object rather than another caption.

The date is unmistakable; less open black space and more visual structure.

Daylight
