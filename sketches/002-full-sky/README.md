## Variant: Full-face sky

### Design stance
The entire 200×228 face is space. The globe and the time sit in it. Year-stars may land in HUD corners; extra dim motes fill every remaining black pixel.

### Key choices
- Layout: 52 year-stars across map void + HUD margins, with a hard keep-out around numerals
- Typography: same HUD, punched out of the starfield
- Color: year-stars as in the constellation; extra motes always navy
- Interaction: day-of-year slider

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: filling the rest of the screen; more “object in space”
- Weak at: time/date can feel less like an instrument panel; motes near 14:39 will fight Leco at real size

### Best for
A more cinematic face if we accept a slightly busier lower third.
